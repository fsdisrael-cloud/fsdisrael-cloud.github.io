01 FLY & LOOK

Drag the stick to steer. Slide the throttle for speed. Drag empty sky to look. TILT enables phone steering.

02 LOCK & FIRE

Quick chirps mean acquiring; a steady tone means locked. Tap MISSILE. Hold GUN for short bursts. Hits are not guaranteed.

03 STRIKE & EVADE

Align the bomb impact mark on an orange target; tap BOMB. Use FLARES against missiles. Hold BRAKE for a tighter turn.

04 HULL & FIVE LIVES

HULL heals very slowly. Five aircraft last the whole run. Stay clear of terrain and keep enough speed to avoid a stall.

05 LAND & RESTORE

AUTO LAND assists runway or friendly carrier approach. Correct gently. Stop fully to refuel, rearm and heal. Leave at READY.

07 CLEAR THE LEVEL

Finish the listed objective quotas for WIN LEVEL. More objectives arrive in later levels. Extra enemies are not always required.

08 BOSS · EVERY 5 LEVELS

Destroy all four weapon pods, then finish the core. Avoid its rear laser warning. It also fires guns, missiles and flares.
